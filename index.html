01 · About关于

Policy, Firms & Networks政策、企业与网络

My research sits at the intersection of U.S.–China industrial policy rivalry and overseas Chinese networks: 我的研究主要围绕中美产业政策博弈与华人华侨网络两条主线展开：

  • How industrial policy shapes firms' innovation and internationalization decisions, and how geopolitical friction reshapes firm behavior and the global supply chain landscape. 考察产业政策如何影响企业创新与国际化决策，以及地缘政治摩擦如何重塑企业行为与全球供应链格局。
  • The historical formation of overseas Chinese networks and their long-run economic impact — systematically turning clan genealogies and migration archives into quantifiable data to identify their role, and economic consequences, in the cross-border flow of capital, trade, and knowledge. 研究华人华侨网络的历史形成及其长期经济影响，通过将宗族族谱、移民档案等历史资料系统转化为可量化、可分析的数据，识别华人华侨网络在资本、贸易与知识跨境流动中的作用及其经济后果。
