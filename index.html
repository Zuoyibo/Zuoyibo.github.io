01 · About关于

Innovation, Policy & AI Methods创新、政策与AI方法

My research sits at the intersection of innovation economics, large-language-model-based tools, industrial policy, and firm internationalization: 我的研究主要围绕创新经济学、AI工具（LLM）应用与实践、产业政策与创新、企业出海四条主线展开：

  • Innovation economics — how government-guided funds and other policy instruments drive firms' substantive innovation. 创新经济学：考察政府引导基金等政策工具如何驱动企业的实质性创新。
  • LLM-based tools and practice — developing and applying large language models to identify and measure industrial policy text at scale. AI工具（LLM）应用与实践：开发并应用大语言模型方法，对产业政策文本进行规模化识别与测量。
  • Industrial policy and innovation — systematically identifying the structure and composition of industrial policy instruments and their effects on firm innovation. 产业政策与创新：系统识别产业政策工具的结构与组合，考察其对企业创新决策的影响。
  • Firm internationalization — how geopolitical friction and restrictive industrial policy reshape firms' internationalization paths and the global supply chain landscape, driving Chinese firms' outward expansion. 企业出海：研究地缘政治摩擦与限制性产业政策如何重塑企业国际化路径与全球供应链格局，推动中国企业的出海决策。
